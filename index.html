This demo: app in English, learning German, for Jamie Collins (sales, Genève, first language English). Every lesson is open; Online courses, e-learning modules, virtual classes, My words and the interactive notebook (grammar of your trip with exercises, verb tables, PDF) are in the JC menu; notifications sit behind the bell; managers and HR find the team dashboard in the menu.
Clickable demo. Colleagues, timetables and progress are sample data. Audio uses the device's synthetic voices; the product would use recordings by native and dialect speakers. Colours follow the CFF palette (red, white, greys) on a dark futuristic base; no CFF logo, font or other brand asset is used.
